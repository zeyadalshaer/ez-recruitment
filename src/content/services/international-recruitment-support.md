Recruiting across borders adds complexity: different labor markets, unfamiliar candidate backgrounds and more steps to coordinate. We support employers through the recruitment side of that process, from defining the requirement to presenting candidates who are suitable for the role.

The value to you is a clearer, better-organized hiring effort, so your team can focus on running the business while the search is managed in a structured way.

Every recruited worker comes with a two-month replacement guarantee in the event of absconding, and each workforce group includes an English-speaking communication representative to help facilitate clear communication between workers and the employer.
