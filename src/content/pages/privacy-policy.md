This Privacy Policy explains how EZ Recruitment ("we", "us") handles information submitted through ez-recruitment.com.

## Information we collect

When you submit the contact form or the partnership inquiry form, we collect the details you provide. Depending on the form and the type of inquiry, this may include your name, company name, position, business email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company website, country, type of recruitment business, areas of interest, industry, job categories, number of workers required, expected timeline, workforce requirements and your message. We also record that you agreed to this Privacy Policy when submitting the form.

This website is for businesses. We do not collect applications from individual job seekers.

## How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, to discuss your requirements or a possible cooperation, and to manage any resulting business relationship. We do not sell your information.

## How it is processed

Form submissions are transmitted securely through our website host and an email delivery service (Resend) to our business email inbox. The website is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process technical data such as IP addresses in order to deliver and protect the site.

## Retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as it is needed to respond to you and to maintain our business records, and then delete it.

## Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To do so, email zeyad@ez-recruitment.com.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page.

## Contact

For any questions about this policy, email zeyad@ez-recruitment.com.
